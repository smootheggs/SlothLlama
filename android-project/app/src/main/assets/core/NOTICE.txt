Core words lists (fr.json, zh.json, yue.json)

French: word frequencies from wordfreq 3.1.1 (data CC BY-SA 4.0, https://github.com/rspeer/wordfreq).
Word list, meanings, notes and conjugation written for SlothLlama.

Mandarin: words, ranks, parts of speech, HSK levels and measure words from complete-hsk-vocabulary
(MIT, https://github.com/drkameleon/complete-hsk-vocabulary); meanings from CC-CEDICT
(CC BY-SA 4.0, https://cc-cedict.org), some rewritten; frequencies from wordfreq (CC BY-SA 4.0).

Cantonese: words, counts, parts of speech and Jyutping from the Hong Kong Cantonese Corpus
(HKCanCor, CC BY 4.0; Luke and Wong 2015); meanings from CC-CEDICT (CC BY-SA 4.0) or written
for SlothLlama.
